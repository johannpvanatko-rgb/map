Luxeum

Grand city

Otherwise known as the Wisdom of the North, Luxeum is the centerpoint of knowledge and home to many scholars, its grand library being famous far and wide. Located on a mountain bordering the Silent sea, it is covered in a snowy blanket year-round. The mountain it sits on also acts as a grave for a giant dragon skeleton. Luxeum welcomes anyone curious, no matter their species and race.

The Pit

Far into the southern ocean, the ocean floor begins to sink into one central point. Believed to be the deepest part of the world, noone, not even the hardiest sea dragons have ever wondered into its depths.

The Conjunction

As one approaches the center of the spiraling islands, chunks of land begin to float, eventually ending up in a mass of floating rocks high in the sky, held by the powerful current of magic in this region

The sheer amount of magic in this area has affected everything in and around it. Overrun with twisted magical predators and strange phenomenon, few have actually ventured here.

Northwatch

Outpost

Not only the farthest outpost to the north, but also the farthest of any settlements. Northwatch serves the purpose of overwatching Eterfrost, reporting anything unusual to its parent city, Luxeum

Due to its conditions, some species cannot access it at all, and it is thus mainly lead by hardy dragons unfazed by the cold.

Outposts

Small in size and located in dangerous uncharted lands, outposts serve as a station of safety to many researchers and adventurers.

Only ever housing a few dozen people at most, these far-away settlements watch over difficult to reach places, researching them in service to larger cities.

Grand Cities

Wealthy, large and renown. Grand cities are beacons of civilization, the centerpoints of trade and knowledge and a home to many different people from many different backgrounds and species.

Their power reaches beyond the city border and they often act as the main city of the nearby land.

Grand cities also have the right of creating smaller outposts in remote locations, even in lands that would otherwise not be considered under their jurisdiction.

Towns

A settlement that has over time grown in size and power. Most towns belong in one of the grand cities jurisdiction, although they may not necessarily need to be connected to them by law, they still depend on them in trade and safety.

Towns come in many sizes and could be interracial or ruled by a single species.

Settlements

Villages and not-quite towns. Settlements are small in size and could even be made up of just a few houses.

Their number greatly overshadows both grand cities and towns and can even exist far outside any jurisdiction, their remoteness sometimes rivals that of outposts and a few of them even serve a similar purpose.

Many settlements are made up of a single species.
